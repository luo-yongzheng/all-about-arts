# Direct a film for this subject

Read the selected `vendor/lemo-opuscar/styles/<slug>/STYLE.md` for the medium’s invariants and the full [directing guide](../vendor/lemo-opuscar/DIRECTOR.md). User choices about silence, slow pacing, format, assets and approval take precedence over generic quotas in that source guide.

## Brief and direction

Identify the audience, intended change in understanding or feeling, factual sources, essential material and format. Resolve only consequential missing information. When the user delegates the choice, choose and continue. Native defaults: 30–60 seconds, 1920×1080, 24 fps unless the style specifies otherwise. Preserve existing project specifications.

When no style is named, compare two or three actual media: continuous marker drawing, transmitted-light paper, measured physical objects, documentary montage or typographic choreography. Choose for the subject’s action and evidence, not the convenience of an eight-style starter. Consider diversity across recent projects when available, while preserving an explicitly requested brand or style.

Pick one or two topic-relevant benchmarks. Record what to learn (framing, rhythm, material, sound) and what remains original (subjects, geometry, shots, melody). A benchmark is neither an asset licence nor a sequence to copy.

## Treatment before demo

Write `TREATMENT.md` before opening `DEMO.md` or demo code. Documentary styles may first use the material-gathering tools explicitly named by STYLE.md. Complete these production decisions:

| Part | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Three structures | Three distinct ways the subject could unfold; choose one and explain why. |
| Logline and arc | Subject, initial situation, consequential change and ending. An explanation may change the viewer’s mental model. |
| Opening / ending | Specific images and how they relate; a generic title alone is not an opening event. |
| Style contract | Material, palette, type, movement, camera and sound grammar from STYLE.md. |
| Signature shot | A topic-specific action made possible by this medium, at a meaningful turn. |
| Shot list | Time, visible action, framing, angle/camera, transition and reason. |
| Beat / cue map | One timeline for picture, voice, music and foley; accents, holds and pace changes. |
| Sound table | Ambience, material/action foley, instruments and deliberate silence per section. |
| Text / evidence | Script, caption design, reading holds and factual sources in FACTS.md. |

For data, keep units consistent within a chart and label uncertainty. Preserve source provenance in FACTS.md/CREDITS and follow the user’s preferences for on-screen citations. For products, show function or a useful physical detail. Art films may rely on a sustained composition without narration.

## Prove the actual medium

After the treatment, consult the demo’s relevant engine modules. Full native source is the technique reference; legacy wrappers are optional prototypes. Whiteboard supports Board/Camera/Pen/Timeline and stroked lettering; paper-lightbox supports cut geometry, transmitted light, depth and focus. Verify font coverage for the actual script: a demo subset is not a general Chinese font.

Render three story moments including the signature shot. For characters add expressions and key poses. Render a short motion test for the hardest action. Inspect subject scale, depth, lighting, silhouette and typography at final size before extending the film.

Let composition follow the subject: continuous world, full-frame action, layered stage, evidence surface, split screen or a purposeful graphic system. A repeated left-title/right-illustration layout is appropriate only when it serves this film. Generic boxes or festival lanterns do not substitute for the requested subject.

## Picture, sound and rhythm

Use a deterministic shared timeline and seeded drawing. Direct/reverse seek must work without prior-frame state. Use one world-to-screen transform for subject-following effects. Keep cameras and lights smooth when animation is deliberately stepped.

Give meaningful gestures anticipation, action and follow-through; inspect handovers and impacts at 0.2-second intervals. Vary framing and pace for a reason. Build transitions from the medium: eraser, fold, ink bleed or physical transformation.

Score from the style’s instrument palette. Coordinate ambience, foley and music with actions; use silence and J/L cuts where they serve the arc. Sparse or silent films need no arbitrary camera or sound quota. Uniform scene lengths and a music loop are not a cue map.

Reading defaults: subtitles at least 1.8 seconds with a margin after speech. After animated text settles, estimate English letters/15 + 1.5 seconds or Chinese characters/4.5 + 1.5; confirm using native readcheck and viewing. Reduce words before increasing reading pressure.

## Originality and review

Follow [quality.md](quality.md). Compare structure, opening, signature shot, camera path, score shape and ending against the demo; aim for at least four genuinely different dimensions while preserving the medium. Write a concrete comparison. For an explicitly requested reproduction or revision, evaluate fidelity to that request instead.

Storyboard approval is a pause only when requested. Otherwise inspect, fix and continue. Technical validity and a self-assigned score do not establish artistic quality.
